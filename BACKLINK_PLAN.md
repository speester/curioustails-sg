## Tier 2 — Outreach (2–4 hrs/week, weeks 2–8)

1. **HDB/lifestyle content pitches.** SG personal-finance and lifestyle blogs (Seedly community, MoneySmart, DollarsAndSense contributors) cover "cost of dog ownership" — pitch the per-breed price table as a citable data source: "the only SG pet shop that publishes all-in prices for 48 breeds." Data = link magnet.
2. **Pet service partners.** Groomers, vets, trainers, pet cafes near Balestier: reciprocal "recommended partners" pages. 5–10 realistic wins.
3. **The AVS-certified trainer** who does the free lessons: ask for a link from their site ("partner shop").
4. **SPCA / smuggled-puppy angle.** /learn/smuggled-puppies is genuinely useful consumer protection content; pitch to SG pet welfare groups and journalists covering smuggling cases as a buyer-education resource.
5. **HARO/Qwoted-style journalist requests** for pet/SME topics — Nelson & Kim as quoted small-business owners.

## Tier 3 — Own portfolio (careful)

You own maltipoo.sg, pomeranian.sg, corgi.sg, chihuahua.sg, maltese.sg, dachshund.sg, shihtzu.sg, cavapoopuppy.sg, bichon.sg, shibainu.sg, puppysg.com, wgpetfarm.com, puppysingapore.com.

- **Do:** one editorial, in-context link per relevant domain max (breed domain → matching curioustails.sg breed page), only from pages with real content, varied anchors.
- **Don't:** sitewide footers, exact-match anchors everywhere, interlinking the whole network to one target. That pattern is a textbook PBN footprint; keep it sparse and natural or skip entirely.

## Anchor text mix (all tiers)

~50% brand ("Curious Tails"), ~30% URL/natural ("this Balestier pet shop"), ~20% partial ("Cavapoo puppies in Singapore"). Never repeat exact-match "puppy for sale singapore" anchors.
