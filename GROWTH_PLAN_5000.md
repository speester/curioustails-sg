Almost everyone searching these terms in Singapore is shopping.

## 5. The plan

### Lane 1 — puppysingapore.com: protect and compound (low effort)
It is working. The July 22 change tripled it. Do not restructure it, do not re-point its keywords, do not let Curious Tails compete with it.

- Keep the head cluster on puppysingapore.com permanently: "puppy singapore", "puppy for sale singapore", "dogs for sale singapore", "buy dog singapore", "puppies singapore".
- Find out exactly what shipped on 2026-07-22 and whether any of it is unshipped elsewhere. That single change is the highest-ROI documented event in either property's history.
- Expected: 2,900 → 3,200/month on trend alone. Budget 1 hr/week.

### Lane 2 — curioustails.sg owns PRICE and QUALIFY (this is the work)

**2a. Convert the price cluster from page-one to top-three.** 87 queries, positions 4–15.

For each of the 48 breed pages, the price section needs to be the thing the page is *best* at, not a section in the middle:
- A price answer-capsule in the first 100 words that states the real figure as a standalone liftable sentence: "A Corgi in Singapore costs $3,688 to $5,500 at Curious Tails, all-in." Every breed page already carries a first-fold price — verified 48/48 — but it is embedded in a comparison sentence, not phrased as the answer to "how much is a corgi in singapore".
- A dedicated `#price` anchor section answering the four price questions people actually type: what it costs here, what is included, why cheaper than X, what the lifetime first-year cost is.
- FAQ entries matching the literal query strings from GSC — "How much is a Corgi in Singapore?" not "Corgi pricing".

Order of work: sort the 87 queries by impressions, do the top 20 breeds first. Weeks 1–6.

**2b. Build the QUALIFY cluster from near-zero.** 14 queries at median #43 is an empty room.

The pages largely exist (`/first-time-owners/hdb-breeds/`, `/first-time-owners/breed-selector/`, the per-breed HDB sections, `/blog/cavapoo-vs-maltipoo/`) and rank nowhere. Give each breed page an explicit, indexable answer to:
- "Is a {breed} HDB approved?" — you have the real answer per breed
- "Is a {breed} good for first-time owners?"
- "{breed} vs {breed}" — you already have the honest steers
This is the class of query where a real shop that turns customers away outranks a content farm. Weeks 4–10.

**2c. Close the breed-page positional gap.** #12–19 → #6–9, matching puppysingapore.com.

Work already done this session that feeds this: availability schema is now honest (9 InStock / 39 OutOfStock, was InStock on all 48), the feed no longer goes stale, breed pages emit a standalone `Product` instead of a one-item `ItemList`. The remaining known drags:
- **Sibling duplication.** 40–60% of each breed page's body text is repeated verbatim across the other 47. Worst: maltipoo 40.9% unique, maltese 44.9%, bichon-frise 48.7%. The single largest block is the review carousel — the same three Google reviews on 42–44 pages.
- **Reviews.** You have 41 real Google reviews and 3 transcribed. Transcribing 15 more fixes the duplication *and* feeds Lane 3. This is the highest-leverage hour in the whole plan and only you can do it.

**2d. Do not chase these.** Bare breed terms ("corgi", "pug", "golden retriever"). I checked the live SERP for "corgi" in Singapore: Knowledge Graph, Reddit, Wikipedia, AKC, and an insurance startup called Corgi. **Not one Singapore pet shop on page one.** The volume is real and the door is shut.

### Lane 3 — the local pack (the 3 slots nobody in your portfolio holds)

For "puppy for sale singapore", **positions 2, 3 and 4 are a local pack**: Cotton Pups (5.0, 84 reviews), The Lovely Pets (4.5, 237), Dawgs & Co (5.0, 187). Curious Tails has 41 reviews at 5.0 and does not appear.

This is the only lane where the prize is three of the top four slots on a 1,000/month query, and neither of your sites can reach it through content. It needs review velocity, photo cadence, correct category and service area. An ask-at-handover routine is the entire intervention.

Ranked third because it has the least evidence behind it — no GBP audit has been run — not because it is worth least. If Lane 2 stalls, this becomes Lane 1.

## 7. Measurement — one number, checked monthly

Do not track total clicks. Track **high-intent clicks**: GSC queries matching for sale / buy / price / cost / how much / where to buy / breeder / near me / shop, summed across both properties, brand terms excluded. That is 93% of puppysingapore.com's traffic today and the honest denominator for this goal.

Second number, monthly: **the median position of the Curious Tails PRICE cluster.** It is 8.4 today. When it reaches 3, Lane 2 has done its job, and the click target follows arithmetically.

## 8. What this plan deliberately does not do

- **No backlink campaign.** Curious Tails has 5 referring domains, which looks alarming and is the constraint on nothing measured here — woofloof.sg holds #2 for "cockapoo singapore" on 4 referring domains, onlypaws.sg holds #1 for "maltipoo singapore" on 10.
- **No new blog posts.** `/learn/puppy-health/` sits at #86.8 on 32 impressions. The informational tier draws nothing; adding to it before the money pages rank is backwards.
- **No URL restructure.** puppysingapore.com uses `/corgi/` and Curious Tails uses `/puppies/corgi/`. That difference is worth noticing but it is correlation, and migrating 48 ranking URLs on a 7-week-old site to chase it would risk more than it could win.
- **No fight for the head term on Curious Tails.** You already hold #4.9 there with the other domain.
