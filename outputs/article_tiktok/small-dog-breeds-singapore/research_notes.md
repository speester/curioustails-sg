# Research notes

Source article: https://curioustails.sg/blog/small-dog-breeds-singapore
Published 2026-08-21 by Curious Tails (AVS licence AS24J00046, Balestier).

Core claim: in Singapore, breed size does not decide whether a dog can live in
your home. Three things do: the HDB approved list, heat and humidity, and
grooming load.

Most concrete details available:
- HDB allows one dog per flat, from a list of 62 approved breeds.
- Penalty for a non-approved breed: up to $4,000.
- Private housing allows up to three dogs, subject to AVS licensing.
- Curly single coats need a full groom every six to eight weeks in this climate.
- Small breeds at Curious Tails start from $3,288, all-in.
- Corgi and Beagle appear on small-dog listicles constantly (CESAR's SG top-20
  opens with Corgi) but neither is HDB approved.

Chosen anchor: the Corgi contradiction. It is truthful, specific, visual, and
it reverses what the top-ranking articles imply.
