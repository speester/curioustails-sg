Angle chosen: size is not what decides whether a small dog can live in your home here; the HDB list, the heat and the coat are.
Stance: the popular small-dog listicles are wrong for Singapore, because they rank by popularity instead of by what the rules and the climate allow.
Target viewer: someone in an HDB flat, weeks away from choosing a first puppy.
Engagement beat: save trigger (the three-part test) plus a comment seed on the Corgi claim, which people push back on.
Loop plan: opens on a Corgi in a bright HDB corridor, closes on "check the list before you fall in love with a breed", which sends the viewer back to the Corgi.

Hook options:
1. stakes: "Pick the wrong small breed here and you could be fined up to four thousand dollars."
2. surprise: "Corgis are on every small dog list in Singapore. You cannot keep one in a flat."
3. conflict: "Every listicle says Corgi. HDB says no."
4. specificity: "Sixty-two breeds. That is the whole list you get to choose from."
5. open_loop: "There is a reason your favourite small breed is not on the list."

Chosen hook: option 2. Truthful, instantly visual (a Corgi in an HDB corridor),
specific, and it contradicts what the reader has just been reading elsewhere.

Rejected risk: option 1 leads with the fine, which reads as fear bait and
overstates how the penalty usually comes up.
