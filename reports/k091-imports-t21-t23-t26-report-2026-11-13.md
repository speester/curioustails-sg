## Is the imported flow complete end to end (apart from the scheduler)?

Yes for the mechanical chain: an item can now go **approved → next sync writes the public JSON and WebPs → build renders the breed card, the detail page with `PreOrder`, `noindex,follow`, the 4-step reservation wording and the Vets For Pets promise → `/available-puppies/` lists it under “Arriving soon” → a takedown removes it from the feed, purges the images, adds the 301 and the next build deletes the page**, all proven in `reports/k091-t26-fixture-e2e.json`. What is still missing between “approved” and “visible on the real site” is not code: it is the **scheduler** (T25, so someone runs the sync), the **T17 legal sign-off** (so `IMPORTS_AUTOPUBLISH=1` can be turned on), the **GA4 registration** (T24) and the promised **design pass**. Until the scheduler exists, “approved” still needs a human to run one `sync:imports --no-fetch` plus the deploy; the fixture proves that command is the only remaining step.
