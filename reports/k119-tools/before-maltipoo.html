What is a Maltipoo?

A Maltipoo is a cross between a Maltese and a Toy or Miniature Poodle. It is a small, affectionate companion dog, roughly 3 to 6 kg, with a soft low-shedding coat, a people-oriented temperament that thrives on company, and a lifespan of 12 to 15 years.

Maltipoo Temperament: The Companion That Wants to Be With You

Maltipoos are affectionate, playful, and deeply people-oriented. This is a dog that wants to be near you, follows you from room to room, and gives back every bit of attention it gets. For the right home, that devotion is the whole appeal.

Here is the honest part we lead with, from two years of matching puppies to families: a Maltipoo's need for company is the single thing that decides whether the match works. In a home with someone usually around, a Maltipoo is a joy. In an empty home from 8am to 6pm, that same devotion can turn into whining and separation anxiety.

They are gentle with children, sociable, and quick to learn. A Maltipoo that gets company and a settled routine is a calm, easy flatmate.

Can a Maltipoo Be Left Alone During Work Hours?

Honestly, this is where you should pause. Maltipoos cope least well of our small breeds with long stretches alone, because company is what they are built for. If someone is home for most of the day, a family member, a retiree, a helper, or you working remotely, a Maltipoo thrives.

If your home is empty from 8am to 6pm every weekday, we will say so plainly, and often point you to a more independent breed instead. We'd rather talk you out of the wrong match than sell you a puppy that spends its days anxious. See our Cavapoo puppies, which handle alone time more comfortably.

Grooming and Tear-Stain Care in Our Humid Climate

Maltipoos are low-shedding, which means the coat needs your help instead of dropping on its own. Brush 3 to 4 times a week to prevent matting, and book professional grooming every 6 to 8 weeks. Singapore's humidity speeds up matting, so a consistent routine matters more here than in cooler climates.

Tear staining is the Maltipoo-specific one. Light reddish-brown marks under the eyes are common in white and cream coats. A daily wipe with clean water, keeping the face hair trimmed, and steady grooming keep it in check. The brush and grooming tools you need are already in the starter kit.

Maltipoo Health: What We Check Before You Take One Home

Every Maltipoo goes home vet-checked, vaccinated, dewormed, and microchipped. The Maltipoo health points worth knowing are a small few: patellar luxation, a kneecap issue common to small breeds; dental crowding, because small jaws leave little room for teeth; and inherited eye conditions. We say this once, plainly, so you hear it from your seller and not from a forum later.

Feeding is simple: your puppy comes with the small-breed food it is already eating, and any change should be gradual over a week. For portioning guidance see the puppy health checks explained guide.

The Truth About Teacup and Micro Maltipoos

You will see "teacup," "micro," and "smallest in Singapore" Maltipoo listings, often at a premium. We don't sell to that label. Dogs bred to be unusually tiny carry higher risks: fragile bones, dental and breathing problems, low blood sugar, and shorter lives.

A healthy small Maltipoo at 3 to 6 kg is already a compact, flat-friendly dog. If a seller promises a size below that and guarantees it, treat the promise as a warning, not a feature.

Teacup red flags

  • Guaranteed adult weight below 3 kg
  • No mention of the parents' actual size
  • "Teacup" or "micro" priced at a premium

Maltipoo FAQs

01How much does a Maltipoo cost in Singapore?

The Maltipoo price in Singapore ranges widely, from around $3,000 to $9,000 depending on size, coat, and shop. At Curious Tails, Maltipoo puppies are $3,288 to $5,988, and that price already includes the $500+ starter kit, free home delivery and setup, and a free first training lesson with an AVS-certified trainer.

02Is a Maltipoo HDB-approved?

Yes. A Maltipoo is an HDB-approved small breed, so you can keep one in an HDB flat. HDB allows one dog of an approved breed per flat, and we complete the dog licensing for you at purchase, so the paperwork is handled.

03Can a Maltipoo be left alone while we are at work?

Maltipoos are people-oriented and happiest with company, so a full 8am to 6pm alone every weekday is not their ideal. They suit homes with someone around: a family, a retiree, a helper, or remote work. If nobody is home all day, a more independent Cavapoo is often the kinder match, and we'll tell you so.

04How big does a Maltipoo get?

Most Maltipoos reach roughly 3 to 6 kg fully grown, depending on the Poodle parent. Be cautious with anyone advertising a "teacup" or "micro" Maltipoo; sizes below the normal range often come with real health trade-offs, and no honest seller can guarantee them.

05Do Maltipoos have tear stains, and how do I manage them?

Light tear staining is common in white and cream Maltipoos. A daily wipe around the eyes with clean water, keeping the face hair trimmed, and regular grooming keep it under control. The grooming tools you need are already in your starter kit.

06Is there a teacup Maltipoo in Singapore?

Teacup Maltipoo is a marketing term, not a size standard. A Maltipoo bred from a Toy Poodle parent naturally stays at the small end, around 3 kg, but listings that promise an adult far below that cannot honestly guarantee it, and undersized dogs carry real health trade-offs. We sell Maltipoos to the normal 3 to 6 kg range and price them openly.

07Are Maltipoos hypoallergenic?

No dog is truly allergen-free, but the Maltipoo coat is soft and low-shedding, so many allergy sufferers tolerate one better than a shedding breed. If allergies are a concern, visit our Balestier shop and spend time with the puppies before you reserve.

08How long do Maltipoos live?

A Maltipoo lifespan of 12 to 15 years is typical with good care. Choosing a healthy puppy in the normal size range, rather than an unusually tiny one, is one of the simplest ways to protect that.
