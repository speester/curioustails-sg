# SME Interview Pack — Nelson & Kim (Curious Tails owners)

Record on Zoom/Google Meet with transcription ON (or phone voice memo → transcribe).
Aim for 45–60 min, conversational. Push for numbers everywhere (%, $, counts,
timeframes, before/after). When done, export the transcript to `sme/raw/` and
re-run sme-extract.

## Origin story & credentials
1. How did Curious Tails start? What were you both doing before, and what pushed you into puppy retail?
2. Walk me through getting the AVS licence (AS24J00046) — what did the process involve, and what does staying compliant actually require month to month?
3. Roughly how many puppies have you matched with families since opening? How many breeds have you personally handled?

## Breed matching (your core differentiator)
4. Tell me about a time you talked a customer OUT of the breed they came in wanting. What did they want, what did you see, what did they leave with, and how did it turn out?
5. What are the 3 questions you always ask a first-time owner before recommending a breed?
6. Which breed do people most often wrongly think they want — and why is it wrong for them?
7. For each breed you carry (Cavapoo, Maltipoo, Mini Dachshund, Corgi, Shiba Inu, Bichonpoo, Cockapoo): who is the ideal owner, and who should NOT buy it?

## Case walkthroughs (aim for 3–5, numbers each)
8. A memorable match that went brilliantly — the family's situation before, what you recommended, and where they are now.
9. A sale you refused or delayed. What happened?
10. A puppy that fell sick in the first 5 days and came back to be nursed — what happened, what did it cost you, how did the family react?
11. A boarding case — someone who used the free 1-month boarding. How did the handover go?

## Failures & lessons
12. Biggest mistake you've made running the shop. What changed after?
13. A match you got wrong. What did you learn?

## Insider knowledge
14. What do buyers not know about how puppy pricing really works in Singapore? Why can you sell $500–$2,000 cheaper than Wag a Tail / Dawgs & Co / The Lovely Pets?
15. What separates a well-run AVS-licensed shop from a bad one — things a customer could check in 5 minutes?
16. What red flags do you see in unlicensed/online sellers that buyers keep falling for?
17. What do the first 72 hours at home actually look like, and what do new owners always get wrong despite the walkthrough?

## Frameworks & repeatables
18. Do you have a named or repeatable process for the home setup + starter-kit walkthrough? Steps?
19. Your rule of thumb for HDB vs condo households and breed choice?

## Quotables & proof
20. What's the line you find yourselves repeating to almost every customer?
21. Which numbers are you comfortable publishing (puppies matched, repeat/referral %, years, review counts)? Anything you'd rather keep private?
